Quick answers

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Can I download Finesse now?

Yes. Finesse is live on the iOS App Store today. Start your 14-day free trial and connect your first account in a few minutes.

What does it cost?

Finesse includes a 14-day free trial with full app access. After the trial, continue for $3.99 weekly, $7.99 monthly, or $59.99 annually.

Can I explore Finesse before installing?

Yes. Use the interactive demo to preview budgeting, daily recaps, recurring costs, alerts, and spending insights on this website.

Accounts connect through Plaid. Finesse never receives your bank password, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and does not sell your personal information.
