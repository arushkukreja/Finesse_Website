01Frequently asked questions

How do I connect a bank account?

Open Finesse, go to Accounts, and tap Link a bank. Finesse uses Plaid to securely connect — you'll enter your bank credentials directly into Plaid's flow, and Finesse never sees or stores your bank username or password.

How do I disconnect or remove a bank?

In the app, go to Accounts → manage and choose Unlink for the institution you want to remove. You can also revoke Plaid's access independently at my.plaid.com.

My transactions or balances look wrong or out of date.

Finesse syncs from your bank through Plaid, and some institutions update less often than others. Pending transactions can also change their amount or date once they post. Try pulling to refresh in the app first. If a balance or transaction still looks incorrect after a day, email us with the account and an example and we'll investigate.

How do subscriptions and billing work?

Finesse subscriptions are processed by Apple through in-app purchase. To view, change, or cancel your plan, open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Finesse. Refund requests are handled by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Finesse → Cancel Subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. Cancelling stops future charges but does not delete your account.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can request deletion from within the app under Profile → Delete account, or by emailing support@finessefinance.us from your account email. Deleting your account removes your linked bank connections and the financial data we store, subject to the limited legal-retention exceptions described in our Privacy Policy.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Bank connections run through Plaid, so your bank credentials never touch Finesse's systems. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and the app supports on-device Face ID / Touch ID locking. We never sell your data and run no third-party ad tracking. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I forgot my password or can't sign in.

On the login screen, use Forgot password to receive a reset link at your account email. If you signed up with Sign in with Apple, use the same Apple ID to sign back in. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help recover your account.

The app crashed or a feature isn't working.

First, make sure you're on the latest version from the App Store and try restarting the app. If the problem continues, email support@finessefinance.us with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a description (or screenshot) of what happened.

02Requirements

Finesse is an iPhone app and requires a recent version of iOS. It is intended for users who are at least 18 years old and have a U.S. bank account supported by Plaid. An internet connection is required to sync your accounts.
